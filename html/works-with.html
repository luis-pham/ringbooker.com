Common Questions

Common Compatibility Concerns

❓

Do I need to replace my booking software?

No. RingBooker works with your existing tools. Square Appointments, Vagaro, Booksy, and Mindbody all stay in place. RingBooker handles the phone layer — not the scheduling layer.

❓

What if my staff still handle some calls?

That's the default approach. Staff answer calls during business hours. RingBooker activates for overflow and after-hours. There's no requirement to hand everything over to AI.

❓

What if I only want after-hours coverage first?

That's the most common starting point. Configure RingBooker for after-hours only — no other change to your operation is necessary. Expand when you're ready.

❓

Does it work with my current phone number?

Yes. RingBooker works via call forwarding on your existing business number. No number migration required. Learn more →

❓

Does it work the same for nail salons and med spas?

Compatibility applies to all five beauty verticals, but the call types and booking flows differ. Each industry page explains how it fits that specific workflow.

❓

Can I start without any booking software integration?

Yes. Call forwarding and after-hours coverage work with zero booking software integration. RingBooker captures intent and sends summaries to your team — your booking system is untouched.
