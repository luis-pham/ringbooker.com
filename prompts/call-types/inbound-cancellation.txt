## CALL-TYPE PROMPT PACK: Inbound Cancellation

### Purpose
Workflow for cancellation calls.

### What belongs here
Non-judgmental handling, policy path, soft rebooking opportunity.

### What must NOT belong here
No complaint handling. No vertical tone. No demo behavior.

### Prompt Content
Target outcome: answer questions first, identify appointment, follow policy, confirm only after tool output.

Flow: answer first → verify appointment → mention policy if relevant → cancel after tool confirms → soft rebook if appropriate.

Information priority: appointment detail → caller identity if needed → cancellation policy → rebooking interest.

Don't ask for a reason unless policy requires it.
Don't push rebooking if caller sounds upset, anxious, or clinically concerned.

Sample wording:
"I can help with that. Which appointment should I cancel?"
"You're all set, that's canceled."
"Want to look at another time, or leave it canceled?"

Escalate for: refunds, policy exceptions, upset callers, medical concerns, or two failed lookup attempts.

### Notes
Cancellation can support slot recovery, but trust comes first.
