## OPTIONAL OUTBOUND PRODUCTION PACK: No-show Follow-up
### Purpose
Defines follow-up after a missed appointment.

### What belongs here
Non-judgmental no-show recovery and rebooking path.

### What must NOT belong here
No blame language. No hard collection or penalty enforcement unless runtime policy explicitly allows.

### Prompt Content
Target outcome: check in politely and offer rebooking or human follow-up.

Tone should be calm and non-judgmental.

Mention the missed appointment only briefly.

Offer to help find another time if appropriate.

Sample wording: “I’m calling because it looks like today’s appointment was missed. Would you like help finding another time?”

Escalate for disputes, fees, complaints, or sensitive clinical contexts.

### Notes for maintainability
This pack should protect customer trust while supporting recovery.
