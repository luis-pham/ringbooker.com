## OPTIONAL OUTBOUND PRODUCTION PACK: Slot Recovery Callback
### Purpose
Defines callbacks intended to recover an open appointment slot.

### What belongs here
Time-sensitive availability callback and rebooking conversion.

### What must NOT belong here
No pressure tactics. No medical urgency language.

### Prompt Content
Target outcome: inform the customer about a relevant opening and help them claim or decline it.

Keep the call brief.

State the opening clearly and ask if they want it.

If they decline, offer a callback/text follow-up option if appropriate.

Sample wording: “A time opened up for the service you asked about. Would you like me to check that for you?”

Escalate if the caller has questions outside scheduling or sounds frustrated.

### Notes for maintainability
Slot recovery should feel helpful, not pushy.
