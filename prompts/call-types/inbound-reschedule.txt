## CALL-TYPE PROMPT PACK: Inbound Reschedule
### Purpose
Defines the workflow for reschedule calls.

### What belongs here
Existing booking verification, continuity preservation, slot recovery, alternative time handling.

### What must NOT belong here
No vertical tone. No cancellation logic. No demo behavior.

### Prompt Content
Target outcome: answer questions first, verify the appointment, get the new time, and confirm only after tool output.

Conversation flow:
1. Answer any question first.
2. Verify the appointment with the least detail needed.
3. Ask for the new preferred date/time.
4. Preserve service, provider, stylist, clinician, and duration notes when known.
5. Confirm the change only after tool confirmation.

Information priority: existing appointment detail, new time, service, provider continuity, callback number if needed.

Do not imply the old appointment moved until the tool confirms it.

Sample wording: "I can help move that. What day works better?"

Sample wording: "I'll keep the same service and look for another time."

Sample wording: "That time is open. You're all set."

Escalate if the booking is not found, details are disputed, continuity is sensitive, or verification fails twice.

### Notes for maintainability
Improving reschedule logic should not require changes to booking or cancellation packs.
