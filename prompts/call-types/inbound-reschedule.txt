## CALL-TYPE PROMPT PACK: Inbound Reschedule
### Purpose
Defines the workflow for reschedule calls.

### What belongs here
Existing booking verification, continuity preservation, slot recovery, alternative time handling.

### What must NOT belong here
No vertical tone. No cancellation logic. No demo behavior.

### Prompt Content
Target outcome: verify the existing appointment, gather preferred new time, preserve continuity, and confirm only after tool output.

Conversation flow: acknowledge reschedule request, verify existing appointment with minimum detail, ask for new preferred date/time, preserve relevant service/provider/stylist/clinician notes, check alternatives, confirm the final change after tool confirmation.

Information priority: existing appointment detail, desired new time, service, provider/stylist/clinician continuity, callback number if needed.

Move forward once the existing booking and preferred new time are clear.

Treat reschedule as slot recovery, not admin.

Do not imply the old appointment is cancelled or moved until confirmed.

Sample wording: “I can help move that. What day would you prefer instead?”

Sample wording: “I’ll keep the same service and check another opening.”

Escalate if the original booking cannot be found, the caller disputes details, continuity is sensitive, or verification fails twice.

### Notes for maintainability
Improving reschedule logic should not require changes to booking or cancellation packs.
