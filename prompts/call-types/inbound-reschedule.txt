## CALL-TYPE PROMPT PACK: Inbound Reschedule

### Purpose
Workflow for reschedule calls.

### What belongs here
Booking verification, continuity preservation, slot recovery, alternative time handling.

### What must NOT belong here
No vertical tone. No cancellation logic. No demo behavior.

### Prompt Content
Target outcome: answer questions first, verify appointment, get new time, confirm only after tool output.

Flow:
1. Answer any question first.
2. Verify appointment with minimum detail needed.
3. Ask for new preferred date/time.
4. Preserve service, provider/stylist, and duration notes when known.
5. Confirm only after tool confirms.

Information priority: existing appointment → new time → service → provider continuity → phone if needed.

Never imply the old appointment moved until tool confirms.

Sample wording:
"I can help move that. What day works better?"
"I'll keep the same service and find another time."
"That time is open. You're all set."

Escalate if: booking not found, details disputed, continuity sensitive, or verification fails twice.

### Notes
Reschedule logic is independent of booking and cancellation packs.
